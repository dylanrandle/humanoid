Modified 2026-11-14: converted COLLADA through binary STL to indexed OBJ for MeshCat/Pinocchio
and MuJoCo compatibility. Concatenated all 18 geometry instances' indexed
triangles, preserving source coordinates, dimensions in meters, winding,
and the original mesh frame. Facet normals follow triangle winding. No simplification.
OBJ omits the source's uniform gray material; the URDF supplies silver.
Triangle count: 231186.

Placement and nominal optical offsets follow upstream _d435.urdf.xacro
at the same revision. Optical offsets are CAD estimates, not measured
calibration.

OBJ preserves all triangles and vertex coordinates while avoiding MuJoCo's
200,000-face STL decoder limit. Repeated vertices are indexed once.

The main URDF uses this OBJ for both display and housing contact.
